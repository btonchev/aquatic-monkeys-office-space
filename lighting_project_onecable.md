## Configuration

**4 × Häfele Spot 9 + 3 × Häfele Spot 4 TT**

| Luminaire   | Quantity | Function                                                                   |
|-------------|---------:|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Spot 9, 80° |        4 | General room lighting                                                      |
| Spot 4 TT   |        3 | Directional lighting for tables, the blackboard, and individual work areas |

**Proposed total power: 79 W**

- 4 × Spot 9 × 16 W = 64 W
- 3 × Spot 4 TT × 5 W = 15 W

The power supply must be compatible with the total load and the OneCable
system limitations.

## Layout on the Fixed Track

The track geometry remains unchanged:

- **Rail C:** upper horizontal section.
- **Rail B:** middle horizontal section.
- **Rail A:** lower horizontal section.
- Rail C and Rail B are connected by a vertical section on the left.
- Rail B and Rail A are connected by a vertical section on the right.
- The power feed is on Rail B, near the right-hand luminaire.

### Luminaire Distribution

## Four Operating Setups

| Parameter          | Conference                                           | Classroom                              | Shared Space                                        | Yoga / Dry Exercises                                                             |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Rail C             | 2 × Spot 9 — general lighting                        | 2 × Spot 9 — lighting over the tables  | 2 × Spot 9 — general workspace lighting             | Spot 9s dimmed to a low level                                                    |
| Rail B             | 3 × Spot 4 TT — directed at the table and blackboard | 3 × Spot 4 TT — directed at the tables | 3 × Spot 4 TT — directed at individual workstations | Spot 4 TT switched off, or dimmed to minimum if soft directional light is needed |
| Rail A             | 2 × Spot 9 — general lighting                        | 2 × Spot 9 — lighting over the tables  | 2 × Spot 9 — general workspace lighting             | Spot 9s dimmed to a low level                                                    |
| Spot 9 dimming     | 100%                                                 | 100%                                   | 70–100%                                             | Approx. 10–20% as a starting point                                               |
| Spot 4 TT dimming  | 70–100%                                              | 80–100%                                | Adjusted to suit the desks                          | Off / minimum                                                                    |
| Colour temperature | 3000 K                                               | 4000 K                                 | 4000 K                                              | 2700–3000 K                                                                      |
| Main objective     | Comfort and even illumination                        | Good visibility for work and teaching  | Localised lighting for each workstation             | Soft, subdued light with the central floor area kept visually calm               |
